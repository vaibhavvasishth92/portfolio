Personal build · Operations Analytics Analytics

Delivery Turnaround Analyzer

Decomposes 720 shipments into seven process stages to isolate where order-to-invoice time is lost, attributes every SLA breach to a root cause, and measures the effect of a cross-functional workflow redesign. Built after leading the real version of this analysis, which cut operational turnaround time by 20%.

  • Stage-level cycle-time decomposition across intake, documentation, planning, carrier assignment, dwell, line haul, and invoice closure
  • Root-cause attribution to the stage with the largest excess over its own baseline — not simply the longest stage
  • Pareto analysis separating the vital few causes from the trivial many
  • Before-and-after measurement with the go-live month excluded as a ramp period

Personal build · Portfolio Governance Governance

Portfolio Governance & QBR Pack Generator

Revenue variance decomposition, margin trend analysis, and a generated quarterly business review pack across 12 enterprise accounts and 20 months of synthetic data. Change the quarter and the entire pack — executive summary, account table, variance drivers, watchlist with actions — rewrites itself.

  • Exact variance decomposition: volume effect + yield effect ≡ total variance — verified programmatically
  • Margin erosion detection via trailing-6-month OLS regression (slope ≤ −18 bps/month, R² ≥ 0.45)
  • Watchlist with derived flags that evolve across quarters — e.g. "growth at declining margin"
  • A printable QBR deliverable: executive summary in generated prose, variance chart, and watchlist with actions

Personal build · Third-Party Risk Risk

Third-Party Risk Assessment

Tiers vendors by inherent risk across five domains against business criticality, producing the assessment record and proportionate due-diligence requirement a bank's risk function expects on file.

  • Five risk domains: financial viability, operational resilience, compliance, information security, and concentration
  • Exposure model weighting inherent domain risk against business criticality, tiered Low through Critical
  • Free-text notes parsed for risk signals — expired certifications, single-source dependency, audit findings
  • Each tier mapped to a defined due-diligence requirement, from baseline checks to enhanced review with executive sign-off

Personal build · Project Management PM

Implementation Gate Tracker

Fourteen concurrent customer implementations tracked through a four-gate phase framework — Kickoff, Requirements, Configuration, Go-Live — with schedule variance, gate exit criteria, and a milestone burn-down chart. Built after standardizing onboarding into a phase-gated framework at Challenger and delivering 40+ implementation projects at Charger.

  • Phase-gate methodology with defined entry/exit criteria per gate — a gate cannot advance until its criteria are met
  • Schedule variance (SV) calculated per gate and cumulatively, with projects ranked by slippage
  • Milestone burn-down: planned baseline vs actual gate completions over time — the gap is portfolio-level schedule slippage
  • Gate detail view with criteria checklists and contextual assessment — blocked projects get escalation recommendations, at-risk projects get scope/resource review
